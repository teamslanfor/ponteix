Ordering questions

How much notice do you need?

For larger orders, like several dozen, please let us know a day or two ahead. For smaller orders, just give us a call.

How do I pay?

You pay when you pick up your order in store.

Do you take orders on Sunday?

We’re closed Sundays. Send your order any time and we’ll confirm it when we’re open.

Do you have allergy information?

Please ask us about ingredients and allergies before you order. Our kitchen handles common allergens.

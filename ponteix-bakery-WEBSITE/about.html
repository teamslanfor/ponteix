Our story

Made in Ponteix

Ponteix Bakery & Restaurant is run by Edna Cyrenne in the heart of Ponteix, Saskatchewan.

We bake fresh donuts, éclairs, muffins, cookies and buns, and serve hearty homestyle food in the restaurant, from perogies to sandwiches on our own fresh buns.

Whether you’re a local stopping in for your morning coffee or you’re passing through southwest Saskatchewan, come in and say hello.
